# Capability: forms

- **forms.1** - a visitor's `POST /_contact` from a site's own address stores a submission (name, email, message, the page) for that site alone and answers 303 back to the page with `contact=sent` *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.2** - a post from any other origin, from the platform host, or with no origin evidence is refused and stores nothing; `/_contact` is the only state-changing path a site host accepts *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.3** - a post with the hidden field filled is answered `sent` and stores nothing *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.4** - a post sent under 3 seconds after its form was issued, or with a missing, forged or day-old token, stores nothing and answers `again` *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.5** - more than 5 posts an hour from one address to one site are answered `busy` and stored nowhere *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.6** - an empty or over-long field, a malformed email, an entry that is not a published entry of this site, or a body over 16 KiB stores nothing (`invalid`, or 413 for the body) *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.7** - one site's submissions are unreadable by every other site and by the app with no site set *Since:* features/19a-receiving-a-message
- **forms.8** - a page or post holding a contact form shows a form with labelled Name, Email and Message fields and a send button, which posts to `/_contact` *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.9** - after a send the visitor returns to the form with the block's thanks line (`sent`) or the fixed line for `invalid`, `again` or `busy`; an unknown answer shows nothing *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.10** - a contact form works with JavaScript off *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.11** - the admin's preview shows the form inert *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.12** - a page or post without a contact form is served without an entry id or token *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.13** - a document can hold one contact form at most *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
- **forms.14** - an admin sees the site's contact messages, newest first, in Messages, with read and unread state *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
- **forms.15** - an admin marks a message read or unread, and it works with JavaScript off *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
- **forms.16** - only an admin of the site reaches Messages; editors and authors get Not Found *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
- **forms.17** - a message is emailed to the address set in Settings, or to every active admin when none is set, with Reply-To the visitor *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
- **forms.18** - a message is kept and shown when its email fails, with a line saying so *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
- **forms.19** - with no email on the installation a message is kept and Messages says so, and nothing is sent *Since:* features/19c-messages-and-the-email
