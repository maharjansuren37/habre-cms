# Capability: editor

- **editor.1** - a page or post is one document of 1-30 sections, each of 1-3 columns of blocks *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.2** - text can be Text, Heading, Subheading or Small heading - and on a page, its one Main heading - a bulleted or numbered list, or a quote, with bold, italic and links *Since:* features/14a1-the-document · *Changed:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.3** - Undo and Redo cover every change, including adding, removing and re-columning sections *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.4** - typing or deleting never merges or crosses sections and columns *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.5** - a link outside the allowlist is refused beside the Web address field *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.6** - a save over the size limit is refused with a message and the text stays in the editor *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.7** - a page or post can hold an image chosen from Media, a button, a line and a space *Since:* features/14a2-image-button-line-and-space
- **editor.8** - focusing a palette tile and pressing Enter adds that block after the selected block, in its column *Since:* features/14a2-image-button-line-and-space
- **editor.9** - an image, a button or a section's settings are edited in a settings panel beside the canvas; text has none *Since:* features/14a2-image-button-line-and-space
- **editor.10** - a button links to a page, a web address or an email address, and an address outside the allowlist is refused beside its field *Since:* features/14a2-image-button-line-and-space
- **editor.11** - an image whose file was deleted shows "This image is no longer available." with Replace image *Since:* features/14a2-image-button-line-and-space
- **editor.12** - the selected block has a ring and a toolbar; Move up and Move down move it through every column and section in reading order, and the new position is announced *Since:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.13** - Delete in the toolbar removes the selected block, says so, and Undo restores it *Since:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.14** - an empty column in a section of two or three columns shows "Empty column - add a block or change Columns in Section settings." in the editor only *Since:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.15** - choosing Main heading on a page makes the previous main heading a Heading and announces it; a page never holds two, and a post offers none *Since:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.16** - a section moves up or down from its chip, and its new position is announced *Since:* features/14a3-block-toolbar
- **editor.17** - a palette block can be dragged into any column - between blocks or at either end - with a gap labelled "Drop <kind> here" where it will land, the place announced, and one Undo removing it *Since:* features/14b-palette-and-drag-and-drop
- **editor.18** - the selected block can be dragged by its toolbar's grip to any place a palette block can go, as one undo step; Escape or a release outside the page cancels, and a release on its own place changes nothing *Since:* features/14b-palette-and-drag-and-drop
- **editor.19** - Duplicate in the toolbar puts a copy after the selected block, selects it and says so, and Undo removes it; a page's main heading's copy is a Heading *Since:* features/14b-palette-and-drag-and-drop
- **editor.20** - a page or post holding an image with no file chosen saves; the image shows "No image chosen" in the editor and nothing on the public site (refused before, with "An image needs a file from Media.") *Since:* features/14c1-ready-made-sections
- **editor.21** - the palette's Sections - Blank section, Hero, Image and text, Call to action - each add an ordinary section of blocks directly after the section being edited, by click, Enter or the phone's Add, announced and undone in one step *Since:* features/14c1-ready-made-sections
- **editor.22** - a hero's heading is the page's Main heading when the page has none, otherwise a Heading; on a post always a Heading *Since:* features/14c1-ready-made-sections
- **editor.23** - in the editor, an empty heading shows its style's name and an empty paragraph beside other blocks shows "Text" *Since:* features/14c1-ready-made-sections
- **editor.24** - a palette section tile can be dragged to before, between or after sections, with a gap labelled "Drop <name> here" where it will land, the place announced, the section added there and one Undo removing it *Since:* features/14c2-dragging-a-section-tile
- **editor.25** - a drag released over anything drawn over the writing area - the sticky top bar, or the settings sheet below 1024px - cancels, announced "Drag cancelled.", and changes nothing *Since:* fixes/drag-backlog-tidy
- **editor.26** - a touch on a palette tile never starts a drag; a tap on it adds its block after the selected one *Since:* fixes/drag-backlog-tidy
- **editor.27** - after a drag is cancelled, the next click anywhere acts normally, even when the gesture ended in pointercancel *Since:* fixes/drag-backlog-tidy
- **editor.28** - while the window auto-scrolls under a drag, a place is announced only once it has held for 300 ms, so no two announcements come under 300 ms apart *Since:* fixes/drag-backlog-tidy
- **editor.29** - a page or post has a title in search results and a description under Search and sharing, saved with the entry *Since:* features/17-seo-basics
- **editor.30** - a page or post can have an image when shared, chosen from this site's Media; another site's image is refused *Since:* features/17-seo-basics
- **editor.31** - the palette's Contact form tile adds the block like any other (click, Enter, drag), with button text and thanks message in the settings panel *Since:* features/19b-the-contact-form-block
