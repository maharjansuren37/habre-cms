# Capability: editor

- **editor.1** - a page or post is one document of 1-30 sections, each of 1-3 columns of blocks *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.2** - text can be Text, Heading, Subheading or Small heading, a bulleted or numbered list, or a quote, with bold, italic and links *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.3** - Undo and Redo cover every change, including adding, removing and re-columning sections *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.4** - typing or deleting never merges or crosses sections and columns *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.5** - a link outside the allowlist is refused beside the Web address field *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
- **editor.6** - a save over the size limit is refused with a message and the text stays in the editor *Since:* features/14a1-the-document
