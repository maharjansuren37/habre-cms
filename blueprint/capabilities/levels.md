# Capability: levels

- **levels.1** - a site is Simple or Pro, Simple by default, and an admin switches it in Settings at no cost *Since:* features/20-simple-and-pro
- **levels.2** - in Simple the Users link is hidden and `/admin/users` is Not Found; in Pro they work *Since:* features/20-simple-and-pro
- **levels.3** - Pro offers Heading 5 and Heading 6; in Simple a block already at H5 or H6 shows its style read-only with "Switch to Pro to change", its text, moves and delete still work *Since:* features/20-simple-and-pro
- **levels.4** - switching from Pro to Simple keeps every page rendering and loses no stored value *Since:* features/20-simple-and-pro
