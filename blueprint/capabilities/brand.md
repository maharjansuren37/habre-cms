# Capability: brand

- **brand.1** - a site's brand colour and style are set in Settings, with No colour (black and white) and Modern the default *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.2** - whatever brand colour is saved, the public site's primary, its hover, the text on it and its subtle background meet WCAG 2.2 AA *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.3** - public pages use the site's brand colour and style preset's fonts and corners *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.4** - a site without a logo shows its name in the brand colour and the preset's heading font *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.5** - Settings previews a brand colour and style before they are saved *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.6** - the editor's canvas shows the site's brand colour and style, its selection chrome Habre's *Since:* features/18a1-brand-colour-and-style
- **brand.7** - Settings uploads a logo - JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or SVG, up to 10 MB - and it counts toward the site's storage *Since:* features/18a2-your-logo
- **brand.8** - an SVG logo is turned into a PNG in the browser, and no SVG reaches the server *Since:* features/18a2-your-logo
- **brand.9** - Settings offers up to three colours from the logo as brand colour swatches, named for their colour *Since:* features/18a2-your-logo
- **brand.10** - choosing, changing or removing a logo never changes the selected brand colour *Since:* features/18a2-your-logo
