# F-255 - A blocked or busy current-password check on Your account logs no security event

**Found:** 2026-10-05 by review (item 15e1; scope: current; lens: every lens; reviewer subagent)
**Why it matters:** Login logs `login_rate_limited` for the same lock; here someone with a stolen session hammering the current-password field leaves no trace once the lock engages (OWASP Top 10:2025 A09).
**Suggested fix:** Log on the blocked and busy branches - `account_password_refused` with a `reason`, or `login_rate_limited` with its scope.
