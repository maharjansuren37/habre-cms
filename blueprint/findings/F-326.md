# F-326 - Shrinking to the 64px sample blends wordmark strokes with an opaque background, giving washed-out or near-white colours

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: current, commit caf4ef2; every lens; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** The default resize kernel mixes each thin stroke with its background, so group averages drift off the real colour or are blends no halo test catches; 18b preselects `colours[0]`. Real sharp runs: thin red+blue text on white gives `["#eae4e7","#8bbad9","#da8b83"]`; "habre" in `#2f6b5a` gives `["#3f7667"]`. The same logos on transparent are right today.
**Suggested fix:** `kernel: "nearest"` on that resize (gives `["#2a81b9","#c13c2f"]` and `["#2f6b5a"]`); a sharp-level test in `optimise-image.server.test.ts` with a thin-stroke wordmark on white; re-run the block fixture.
