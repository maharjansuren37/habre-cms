# F-341 - A timeout can still go uncharged through retries, failover or a slow configured model

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: c59b1aa; lens: every lens; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** Split from F-337 by review: at ~72 tokens/s one 8000-token attempt takes ~110 s, but the SDK's two retries on 408/409/429/5xx, OpenRouter's invisible upstream failover, or a model set in `OPENROUTER_MODEL` at ~25 tokens/s can still reach 5 minutes. Cost-less responses and `interrupted` runs also charge 0.
**Suggested fix:** `maxRetries: 1`, or charge a timeout an estimate; or the owner accepts or defers it (e.g. to 18b2's measurement), recorded with a reason.
