# F-428 - Signup would open to strangers with no email provider and no terms or privacy policy

**File:** `blueprint/context/needs-you.md` (Resend, terms of service and privacy policy, licence lines)
**Found:** 2026-10-12 by preflight
**Why it matters:** v1's point is signup. `SIGNUP_MODE` other than `off` needs `SMTP_URL` and `EMAIL_FROM`, so a Resend account with SPF, DKIM and DMARC records on a sending domain must exist first (also needed for password reset, contact notifications and email change). The app stores members' names and emails and visitors' contact messages and sends descriptions to AI providers; there is no terms of service or privacy policy, no statement of backup retention for deleted accounts (the R2 bucket's retention is unrecorded), and no LICENSE file. `needs-you.md` says all of these are needed before signup opens. A production OpenRouter key with a credit limit is also still to be created.
**Suggested fix:** The owner: create the Resend account and records, write and review the two policies, check the R2 backup retention, choose AGPL or MIT. `host` enters the production values; `docs` adds the policy pages and LICENSE.
