# F-139 - The 16 KB settings cap can refuse a valid save with non-Latin labels

**Found:** 2026-10-02 by review (independent `reviewer` agent; scope: current, tidy-findings; lens: all)
**Why it matters:** Labels allow 100 characters; a 3-byte UTF-8 character percent-encodes to 9 bytes, so 20 such labels are ~18 KB before the title. `url` has no length cap. A refused save shows a bare 413 page and the draft is lost.
**Suggested fix:** Derive the cap from the real field limits (e.g. 64 KB) and give `url` a max length in `navigationSchema`.
