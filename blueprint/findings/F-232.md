# F-232 - "Check your email" says "We sent a link" above "We couldn't send the email"

**Found:** 2026-10-04 by verify (item 15b, Mailpit-stopped check; screenshot `mailpit-stopped-390.png` in the session scratchpad)
**Why it matters:** With `?failed=1`, `/signup/sent` keeps its normal paragraph, "We sent a link to <email>. Open it to confirm your address…", and adds the error `Message` "We couldn't send the email." under it. The page tells the person both that the link was sent and that it was not. The spec asks only that the page also shows the error, so the build met the letter of it.
**Suggested fix:** in `app/features/signup/signup-sent.tsx`, when `failed` is set and no action has answered yet, replace the paragraph with one that names the address without claiming a send - e.g. "Your account is ready. We couldn't email <email> a link yet." - and keep the error `Message` and Send it again.
