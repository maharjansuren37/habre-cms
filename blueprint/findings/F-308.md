# F-308 - Each upload decodes the original five times

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: current; every lens; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** Every size and the social JPEG starts from `upright(bytes)`; PNG has no shrink-on-load. ~3 s per 24 MP on the dev machine, more on 2 vCPUs.
**Suggested fix:** If the time matters: decode once to the widest target and derive the smaller sizes from that buffer.
**Resolution:**

**Update 2026-10-07 (review of 18a2):** the 64px palette read adds a sixth decode of the original per upload.
