# F-104 - `post.tsx` still has its own copy of the image lookup that `resolvePageMedia` now shares

**File:** app/features/public-site/post.tsx:21-34; app/features/public-site/page-media.server.ts:13
**Found:** 2026-09-28 by review (scope: current; lens: quality)
**Why it matters:** Item 9 moved `page.tsx`'s image lookup into `resolvePageMedia` so `/` and `/:slug` share it. `post.tsx`, the third consumer, was outside the spec's files and still builds `mediaById` inline. A change to how images resolve, such as a new field, now has to be made in two places.
**Suggested fix:** Have `post.tsx` call the shared helper. That probably means renaming it to something like `resolveBlockMedia`, since it is no longer only for pages. Make it a small refactor item of its own.
**Resolution:** closed 2026-10-02 by review (item 14a1, independent `reviewer`, scope current, every lens) - page-media.server.ts deleted; post.tsx has no image lookup.
