# F-144 - The left edge of Log out's focus ring is clipped on wide screens

**Found:** 2026-10-02 by review (independent `reviewer` agent; scope: current, item 13b; lens: all)
**Why it matters:** The negative margin puts the button at x=0, so its 2px ring at 2px offset falls off-screen and inside the sidebar's overflow clip; three sides still show (2.4.7 met). The focus test reads computed outline only, so it cannot see this.
**Suggested fix:** `outline-offset: -2px` on `.user .logout` at wide widths, or drop the negative margin and align the label another way.
