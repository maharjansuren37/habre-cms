# F-296 - The main address's tie-break (earliest verified, then by name) has no test, though item 21 relies on it

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: current; lens: tests)
**Why it matters:** `ownDomainSql` orders by `verified_at, domain`; that rule picks every canonical link, the sitemap's address and the 301 target, and the spec names it as a contract item 21 builds on. The only test (`seo.spec.ts:99`) gives a site one domain, so dropping or reversing the `order by` passes every test.
**Suggested fix:** In `e2e/seo.spec.ts` on the proxied server, give one site two verified domains - the earlier `verified_at` on the name that sorts later - plus a same-time pair; assert the subdomain's 301 `Location` names the earliest, and on a tie the first by name.
