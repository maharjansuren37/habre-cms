# F-237 - Account pre-hijacking: an invited signup can register someone else's email with a password the attacker knows

**Found:** 2026-10-04 by review (independent `reviewer` agent; scope: current, item 15b, b240716..46f4db2; lens: all)
**Why it matters:** The victim receives "finish creating your website"; pressing Confirm signs them in and confirms the account, and the attacker can log in with their password until the victim changes it (15c/15e). Bounded by invites today.
**Suggested fix:** Before `open` mode or 15c: ask for the password on the confirm page, or invalidate the password at confirmation - or record it as an accepted risk.
