# F-409 - Billing ids have no index or uniqueness: webhook lookups scan `sites` and two sites could hold one subscription

**Found:** 2026-10-12 by review (scope: current; lens: performance, quality)
**Why it matters:** `findSite` filters `billing_subscription_id` and `billing_customer_id`, neither indexed, and nothing stops two rows sharing one; `for update` then locks whichever comes first. Small tables hide it today.
**Suggested fix:** a follow-up migration with partial unique indexes `where billing_subscription_id is not null` and the same for the customer id.
