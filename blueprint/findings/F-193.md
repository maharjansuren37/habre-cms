# F-193 - A release over the sticky top bar while it auto-scrolls drops the block into a section hidden under the bar

**Found:** 2026-10-03 by review (scope: current; lens: every lens; item 14b, autopilot, reviewer subagent)
**Why it matters:** `slotAt` tests section rects but not the sticky `[data-sticky-bar]` drawn over them: over the bar the status announces a hidden place and a release stores it (reproduced: "Text moved: block 1 of 2 in section 4."). design.md says a release outside every section cancels; to the person the bar is not a section. One Ctrl+Z undoes it.
**Suggested fix:** In `measure()` clip each `sectionRect.top` to the bar's bottom, or treat `y < bar.bottom` as no slot in `update`/`onUp`. Check the bottom-sheet settings panel below 1024px the same way.
**Resolution:** re-examined 2026-10-03 by review (item 14c2): still open, and now reached by section drags too - `sectionSlotUnder` (block-drag.client.tsx:179-187, called at :388 and :458) tests only the writing area's rect, which the sticky bar covers on any scrolled page, so a section tile released over the bar adds a section at a hidden slot. Found by reading, not reproduced.
