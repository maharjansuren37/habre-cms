# F-417 - A paying squatter can hold someone else's domain by removing and re-adding it inside 24 hours

**Found:** 2026-10-12 by review (scope: current; lens: every lens)
**Why it matters:** A claim is replaceable only when 24 h old; remove then add makes a new row, so one Launch site can keep a domain unavailable to its real owner indefinitely. Needs a paid site, so the cost is $19 a month.
**Suggested fix:** Allow several unverified claims for one name (primary key on domain + site), or let a claim that proves its TXT displace an unverified one. A decision for the owner.
