# F-121 - The design record says focus lands on the next section after a remove; the code lands on the previous one when the last is removed

**File:** blueprint/context/design.md (*Sections* entry); app/features/blocks/sections-editor.client.tsx:remove
**Found:** 2026-10-01 by review (scope: current; lens: accessibility)
**Why it matters:** `remove` focuses `next[Math.min(index, next.length - 1)]`, which is the following section except when the last one was removed, where it is the one before. The record is what later items and reviews measure against, and it is slightly wrong.
**Suggested fix:** Reword the record ("the section that takes its place, or the one before when the last was removed").
**Resolution:** fixed 2026-10-01. `design.md` *Sections* now says focus goes to the section that takes the removed one's place, or the one before when the last was removed.
