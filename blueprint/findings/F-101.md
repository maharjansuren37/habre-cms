# F-101 - Two security-event log lines still record the raw `.data` path

**File:** app/root.tsx:50 (`refuseCrossSiteRequests`); app/features/auth/require-role.server.ts:26
**Found:** 2026-09-28 by review (scope: current; lens: quality)
**Why it matters:** Both log `new URL(request.url).pathname`, so a refusal during a client-side navigation is logged as `/admin/users.data` rather than `/admin/users`. That goes against the fix's own recorded decision ("read the path from `url`, never `request.url`") and makes the same route show up under two names in the journal. Only logging is affected; the refusals themselves are correct.
**Suggested fix:** take `url` from the middleware arguments in both places and log `url.pathname`. There's no test to add, since no log assertion exists today.
