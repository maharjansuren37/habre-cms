# F-196 - Dragging a palette tile by touch is likely cancelled by the browser's own panning

**Found:** 2026-10-03 by review (scope: current; lens: every lens; item 14b, autopilot, reviewer subagent)
**Why it matters:** The side panel shows from 1024px, which includes a landscape iPad: past 6px the ghost appears, then the browser's pan fires `pointercancel` and "Drag cancelled." is heard. The spec requires touch only on the grip.
**Suggested fix:** `touch-action: none` on side-panel tiles, or skip `startDrag` for `pointerType === "touch"` so a tap stays a click.
