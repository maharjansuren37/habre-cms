# F-182 - Clicking the Text style button while its menu is open drops focus to the page body

**Found:** 2026-10-03 by review (independent `reviewer` agent; scope: current, 3044513..0bd787a, item 14a3; lens: all)
**Why it matters:** onMouseDown keeps focus on the menu item; the click's setOpen(false) unmounts it, and focus falls to <body> (WCAG 2.2 2.4.3).
**Suggested fix:** When open, call close(true) in onClick instead of setOpen(!isOpen).
**Resolution:** Repaired 2026-10-03 (`block-toolbar.client.tsx`). Test `e2e/block-toolbar.spec.ts` "F-182: pressing the Text style button while its menu is open…", written from this finding's reproduction: failed without the repair, passes with it. Closed by build (P3).
