# F-113 - UptimeRobot watches only the admin hostname; the public site and its certificate have no check

**File:** UptimeRobot account (owner's); `dev-notes/status.md` *Monitoring*
**Found:** 2026-09-29 by monitor
**Why it matters:** Caddy's access logs show 3,628 UptimeRobot requests to `cms.surenmaharjan.fyi/healthz` between 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-28, and none to `surenmaharjan.fyi`. The 11 `/healthz` hits on the public hostname are manual post-deploy curls. Visitors only see the public hostname. A failure that affects only that hostname would reach no one: its Caddy site block, its DNS record, host routing (`app/lib/host-routing.ts`), or its certificate. Its certificate expires first (2026-12-02, against 2026-12-14 for `cms.`), and the SSL-expiry alert covers only `cms.`. `quality-bar.md` says "an uptime check alerts the owner". Today that holds for half the site.
**Suggested fix:** In UptimeRobot, add a second keyword monitor: `https://surenmaharjan.fyi/healthz`, keyword `"database":"up"`, 5 minutes, SSL-expiry alerts on, and the same email contact. That fits the free tier (50 monitors). Prove it by pausing the check or pointing it at a wrong keyword once, and watch for the alert email.
