# F-130 - The hero, call-to-action and image-and-text forms and the image picker dialog have never had a keyboard and screen-reader pass

**Found:** 2026-10-01 by preflight (milestone audit before deploying items 12a-12c)
**Why it matters:** The owner's acceptance of F-125 covers items 10-11 only. 12a-12c add new controls: three more add buttons, the slot forms with required-field hints and errors, the image slot's Choose/Change button, and the picker dialog's focus in and out. The bar is WCAG 2.2 AA (`design.md`); automated tests cover keyboard operation, not what a screen reader announces. Only the owner can accept going without it.
**Suggested fix:** Do the pass (NVDA, VoiceOver or Orca) over a page with a hero, a call to action and an image-and-text section (the `needs-you.md` section-controls line lists what to cover), or record an explicit acceptance with a reason.
**Resolution:** accepted by the owner, 2026-10-01, in conversation: items 12a-12c go to production without the pass. No reason was stated beyond the decision; the pass stays open in `needs-you.md` and `preflight` will report it again.
