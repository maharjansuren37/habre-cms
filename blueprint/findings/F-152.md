# F-152 - A keyboard selection in the new editor is sometimes never registered, so Bold applies to nothing

**Found:** 2026-10-02 by build (item 14a1, step 7, autopilot); in a test step 6 rewrote
**Why it matters:** `e2e/content-security-policy.spec.ts:133` fails about 1-2 runs in 10 (serial, `--repeat-each`): after typing and Shift+Ctrl+ArrowLeft, the TipTap editor's own `state.selection` stays empty for 5 s, so Bold toggles a stored mark and no `strong` appears. Not a short `selectionchange` lag (waiting on the editor's selection did not help), so it may be a real editor defect - a keyboard selection lost or reset - not only test timing. A flaky test in CI also hides real failures.
**Suggested fix:** Run `debug`: at the failure, compare the DOM selection with the editor's; check whether the editor re-renders or resets selection after the title is filled on `/admin/posts/new`. Fix the cause, then prove with `--repeat-each=30`.
**Resolution:** still open, test-only workaround (2026-10-02). debug: uninstrumented 9 of 30 serial runs failed; the editor's selection stays collapsed for 5 s (lost, not slow); the window opens after the typing (a round trip after the click: 14/30 fail; after the typing: 3/30; 1 s wait: 0/20); any in-page recorder hides it (0 of 140), so no failing run was captured. Ruled out: setOptions on re-render (F-158 repaired, still 8/30 failing), ProseMirror's suppressSelectionUpdates and flushSoon (Android/IE/Safari only), editor destroyed or focus lost (passing runs only). Workaround in e2e/content-security-policy.spec.ts: wait for the typed text and two animation frames before selecting - 30/30. Cause unknown: a late write of the editor's stale selection after a keystroke is the most likely explanation, unconfirmed. Real-user impact: a key pressed within a few ms of typing.
