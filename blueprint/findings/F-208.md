# F-208 - Escape, hold over 1 s, then release on the starting tile still clicks it

**Found:** 2026-10-04 by review (scope: current, main..03aa4a8, drag tidy fix; lens: every lens; reviewer subagent)
**Why it matters:** The 1 s fallback (F-195) ends the blocker on time, not on the gesture: a slow release over the tile the drag began on adds its block. Swallowed before this item; a trade-off the spec chose, now that `pointercancel` is handled the timer is only a safety net.
**Suggested fix:** End the blocker on the next capture-phase `pointerdown` instead of after 1 s - a new gesture means the old one is over.
