# F-311 - A year-long immutable cache may keep serving deleted images from a CDN edge

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: current; every lens; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** Once a Cloudflare-cached custom media domain exists, a deleted image or account's images may be served for up to a year; the planned terms say deletion is immediate (OWASP Top 10:2025 A02).
**Suggested fix:** Purge on delete, or record the limit on the custom-domain needs-you line and in the terms.
