# F-298 - An animated WebP is flattened to its first frame, and a long one is refused as over 40 megapixels

**Found:** 2026-10-07 by review (scope: current; every lens; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** WebP is in the upload allowlist and was stored animated before 17b. Only a GIF with `pages > 1` skips resizing, so an animated WebP becomes a still; and its cap is computed on every frame stacked (a 90 KB, 41-frame 1000x1000 WebP comes back `too-large`). The spec only names GIFs - a gap.
**Suggested fix:** Decision needed: treat any `pages > 1` (GIF or WebP) as `as-uploaded` (smallest), or keep stills and compute the cap from `pageHeight ?? height`, saying so in the spec.
