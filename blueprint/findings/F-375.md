# F-375 - Signing in on the way to the preview leaves the brand colour refused until a reload

**Found:** 2026-10-08 by review (scope: current 7fd0e55..774cc02; lens: all; via reviewer)
**Why it matters:** Login's Form follows the redirect client-side, so the document keeps /admin/login's admin CSP (style-src 'self') and the preview's nonce'd brand <style> is blocked. Reproduced 2026-10-08: style-src-elem violation, --color-primary #171717, #aa5500 after reload. Reached from a bookmark or an expired session opening Preview.
**Suggested fix:** In the login action use redirectDocument(destination) when areaForPath(destination) !== "admin"; e2e: sign in from /admin/preview, no CSP violation and the brand colour applied.
