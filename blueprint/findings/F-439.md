# F-439 - Local backups live about 9 days, not the 7 the policy states

**Found:** 2026-10-13 by review (scope: current; autopilot build..review, independent reviewer)
**Why it matters:** `deploy/backup-db.sh:79` deletes with `-mtime +7`, which matches only files 8 whole days old, after the new dump; a dump survives to the 9th nightly run. The privacy policy (retention section) and `needs-you.md` say 7 days.
**Suggested fix:** `-mtime +6` in the script, or state "about 9 days".
**Resolution:** Policy says "up to 9 days on the server"; needs-you.md's terms line says about 9 days and why. Script unchanged (no production behaviour change). Wording only - no test; left for review.
