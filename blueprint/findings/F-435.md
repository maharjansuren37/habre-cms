# F-435 - CAA allowing only letsencrypt.org blocks Caddy's ZeroSSL fallback

**Found:** 2026-10-12 by review (scope: current; lens: security)
**Why it matters:** Caddy falls back to ZeroSSL when Let's Encrypt fails or rate-limits; a CAA naming only `letsencrypt.org` refuses that, most likely to bite on the wildcard on a fresh server. Both domains now carry such a record.
**Suggested fix:** Add `sectigo.com` (ZeroSSL's CA) to `issue` and `issuewild`, or record that the fallback is given up on purpose.
**Resolution:** Owner chose to keep the fallback (2026-10-12): needs-you's hardening line asks for `issue` and `issuewild` `sectigo.com` CAA records on both domains. Not yet added in DNS.
