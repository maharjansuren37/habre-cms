# F-363 - "Two signups at once" can get "Signup is busy" instead of "is taken" when other tests hold the password-check slots

**Found:** 2026-10-08 by build (fix: e2e strict-mode flakes)
**Why it matters:** `e2e/signup.spec.ts:272` expects the losing signup to say "is taken", but signup takes one of the `passwordCheckSlots` it shares with login (`app/features/signup/signup.server.ts:64`); with other signups and logins in flight the loser gets "Signup is busy." (503). Seen 2 of 10 with `--repeat-each=10` on 2 workers; 0 of 10 on one worker. Not yet seen in CI.
**Suggested fix:** `debug` it under CI-like load first; then either run this test in a serial block / retry the losing submit on "busy", or decide the slot count. Same family as F-231 and F-251's sign-in stalls.
**Resolution:** Confirmed in CI 2026-10-08, PR #44 run 37683844073 ("Signup is busy." on the losing page). Repair is Step 2 of the strict-mode fix. Fixed by build 2026-10-08: `raceTwoSignups` runs the race as an attempt and repeats it, at most 3 times, when either page says "busy"; the assertions after it are unchanged. Red before: `--repeat-each=10` on 2 workers, 1 of 20 failed with "Signup is busy". Green after: 20/20, then 60/60 at `--repeat-each=30`; `signup.spec.ts` 24/24. The busy case cannot be forced, so the red run is a random repro - left `fixed` for review.
