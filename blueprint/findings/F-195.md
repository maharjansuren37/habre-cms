# F-195 - The click blocker can outlive a cancelled drag and swallow the next unrelated click

**Found:** 2026-10-03 by review (scope: current; lens: every lens; item 14b, autopilot, reviewer subagent)
**Why it matters:** After Escape or a document change mid-drag the capture-phase click blocker waits only for `pointerup`; a gesture ending in `pointercancel` (touch, capture already released) leaves it until the next press anywhere, whose click (Save, Publish) is eaten. Unmount does not remove it.
**Suggested fix:** Also remove it on a one-time `pointercancel`, plus a fallback timeout (~1s).
**Resolution:** re-examined 2026-10-03 by review (item 14c2): unchanged; the section path calls the same `swallowClick(false)` (block-drag.client.tsx:462).

**Resolution (repair):** fixed by build (autopilot) 2026-10-04 (drag tidy fix, Step 4). `swallowClick(true)` ends on the first of `pointerup` (removal deferred a tick so the release's click is still swallowed), `pointercancel` or a 1 s fallback; each call removes only its own handlers. Test: block-drag.spec.ts "after a drag is cancelled by Escape and its gesture ends in pointercancel, the next click adds a line" - red with the change stashed (`hr` count 0, click swallowed), green 6 runs in a row. Known trade-off of the fallback: Escape, hold over 1 s, release on the same tile still clicks it. Left `fixed` for review.
