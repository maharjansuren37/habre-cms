Web address: /posts/volunteer-orientation-saturdays

Starting in October, new volunteer orientation will run on the first Saturday of every month, from 9:30 to 11:00. Weeknight sessions were hard for many of you to reach after work, and the garden is at its best in the morning light anyway.

What happens at orientation

  • A walk around the plots, the tool shed and the compost bays
  • How to sign up for watering shifts on the board by the gate
  • Safety basics: gloves, tools and the first-aid kit

No need to bring anything but sturdy shoes. If you can’t make the first Saturday, email volunteers@riversidegarden.org or ask anyone in a green apron.
