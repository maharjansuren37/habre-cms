Spring planting day: what to bring

Our spring planting day is on Saturday 3 October, from 9:00 until early afternoon. It is the busiest day of the garden year and the most fun: by lunchtime every raised bed should have something growing in it.

You don’t need any experience. We pair first-timers with someone who has done it before, and there are jobs for every level of energy - from turning compost to labelling seed trays in the shade.

What to bring

  • Sturdy shoes you don’t mind getting muddy
  • A water bottle - there is a tap by the shed for refills
  • Gardening gloves if you have them; we have spares if you don’t
  • Sun cream and a hat

What we provide

Seedlings, tools, compost and a shared lunch. If you have spare seedlings of your own, bring them along to the swap table by the gate.

Questions? Email volunteers@riversidegarden.org and one of us will get back to you within a couple of days.
